Series RC circuit

Capacitor voltage cannot jump. Set the source high to charge the capacitor, then set it to 0 V to let it discharge through the resistor. The loop current reverses. Choose Sinusoidal under Source waveform to try an AC input and adjust its frequency.

The 0 V source setting keeps the loop closed. Playback is scaled to τ, with one τ per second at 1×.
